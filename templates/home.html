One timeline, many roles.

People, tools, and the world around them create events. Choose a moment to see which agents pick it up.

Choose a moment in the timeline

From a person

“Can we move the Friday review?”

Conversation
Asks which time works. The calendar stays unchanged.
Archive
Keeps the request with its original wording and time.

From the workspace

A revised agenda lands in the shared folder.

Workspace
Indexes the new version and keeps the previous one available.
Research
Asks another agent to check which agenda items changed.
Archive
Links the revision to the earlier request.

From the system

The thread is getting too long to replay.

Compaction
Builds a short working brief with links to the exact decisions.
Archive
Keeps the original timeline searchable.
Conversation
Picks up from the brief without asking everyone to start over.

External systems

Conversation
